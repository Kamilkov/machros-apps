Tesouros Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

The short version

Your data is yours. Tesouros has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours. Everything you put into the app stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account.

What Tesouros stores

Collections, items, photos, ratings, notes, tags and dates that you create in the app. This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and synchronized through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database, so it follows you to a new device. We (the developer) cannot access, read, or receive any of it.

What Tesouros does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services beyond what Apple provides with your consent
  • No advertising, no third-party SDKs
  • No data ever sent to the developer or anyone else

Camera and photos

Tesouros asks for camera access only when you take a photo for an item. Choosing photos from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which hands the app just the photos you select — Tesouros never gets access to your photo library. Photos you add are stored with your data as described above and are never transmitted anywhere else.

Backups

Like most apps, Tesouros's local data (including photos and notes) is included in your device backups — iCloud Backup or encrypted computer backups — under Apple's standard backup protections.

Deleting your data

Delete items or collections in the app, or delete the app to remove all local data. iCloud data can be removed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
